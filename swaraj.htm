Dual Platform Architecture

Aerial System

Autonomous Tilt-Wing UAV

A Quad V-tail, tilt-wing drone designed for rapid aerial seeding, liquid spraying, and crop monitoring[cite: 6, 8, 9].

  • Payload Capacity: 15 Liters liquid / 1 kg seeds[cite: 14]
  • Flight Controller: Pixhawk 2.4.8 + ESP32-S3[cite: 14]
  • Dispersal: Servo-controlled precision seed hopping & active liquid spraying[cite: 8, 11]
Ground System

Autonomous Tilling Rover

A track-driven 7 HP petrol-driven ground rover engineered for rotary tilling with reduced energy demands[cite: 6, 12, 14].

  • Capacity: 0.7 – 1.0 Acre per hour[cite: 14]
  • Tilling Mechanism: Rotary spinning blades driven by a planetary gear motor[cite: 12]
  • Power System: Petrol + Solar Hybrid setup[cite: 12]
Core Innovation

Active Liquid Stabilization System (ALSS)

Liquid sloshing during sudden aerial turns alters the center of gravity and reduces flight stability[cite: 10]. ALSS introduces a patentable dual-stage active/passive stabilization framework[cite: 10].

1. Passive Honeycomb

Internal T2 honeycomb compartment structures physically constrain rapid fluid momentum inside the tank[cite: 8, 10].

2. ESP32 Real-Time Control

Reads flight telemetry from the IMU/Pixhawk and processes counter-angles in under 0.05 seconds[cite: 10, 14].

3. Active 2D Servo Mount

Rotates the payload tank across X and Y axes in counter-proportion to drone pitch/roll angles[cite: 10].
